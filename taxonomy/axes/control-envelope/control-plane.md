# Control plane Layer

## Layer Overview

**Axis:** Control envelope (C)

**Layer:** Control plane

**Applies to:** Platform-instantiated systems only

**ID pattern:** `CE.PLANE.n`

**Scope of the layer:** Registration, ownership, and versioning of agents, skills, and tools; required evaluation before publication; human review of agent-proposed changes; staged rollout, rollback, and deprecation; and change propagation with downstream Profile invalidation.

## Scope

Applies to **platform-instantiated systems only**. For a hosted agent, substrate controls are held by reference through the platform anchor and form part of its control envelope. See [Agent-building platforms](../../cross-axis/agent-building-platforms.md).

## Documentation Status

Control names and IDs are fixed in v0.1.0. For each control, the following are open contributions (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#4-control-submission)):

- **Definition**: what the control does and what it does not do
- **Configuration parameters**: what a Profile must record about how it is configured
- **Evidence by assurance level**: what supports a Level 1 assertion vs. a Level 2/3 test
- **Crosswalk**: corresponding OWASP, NIST, and sector-catalogue controls
