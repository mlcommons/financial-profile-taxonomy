# Candidate Use Cases

## Overview

**Axis:** Operational design domain (O)

**Splitting rule:** Line of business.

**Status:** Fifty-two candidate use cases, listed to show the shape of the axis. Preliminary and open; IDs are stable once published and are never reused.

Use-case IDs (`ODD.n`) are the anchor for an ODD instance. A Profile's ODD states the use case(s) together with the deployment's systems, data, and scope; for example, the [alert-triage worked example](../../examples-overview.md#fincrime-alert-triage-agent) scopes `ODD.20` to "retail transaction-monitoring alerts under the institution's financial-crime programme, scoped to structuring and rapid-movement-of-funds typologies."

## Adding a Use Case

Use the [use-case submission](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#3-use-case-submission) process. A proposal should name the line of business, explain why the use case is distinct from existing entries, and describe the typical systems, data, and decisions involved, without disclosing any institution's confidential configuration.
