# Governance

The MLCommons Agent Reliability Profile Taxonomy is maintained by the MLCommons Financial Services Working Group.

The preprint "Agent Reliability Profiles in Financial Services" (Hsu et al., 2026) sets out the four-axis architecture, the design requirements, and the implementation program that guide maintenance. The working group applies that methodology when evaluating contributions.

## Regulatory Observers

Supervisory authorities may participate as observers. Observer participation does not constitute endorsement of the taxonomy or of any Profile described in its terms, and observers do not set technical standards through this repository.

## Conflicts of Interest

Reviewers disclose and recuse where appropriate, consistent with standard MLCommons practice. Reviewers affiliated with a vendor, institution, or assessor recuse from decisions that would specifically advantage their organization's product or deployment.

## Code of Conduct

All contributors and reviewers adhere to the [MLCommons Code of Conduct](https://mlcommons.org/policies/).
